Misfits

In every generation, a few people see the world differently. Most are ignored. A few are resisted. And a rare few quietly change how the rest of us live. The ones who found meaning in scattered stars. The ones who traced a path from ape to man. The ones who imagined a war without weapons. They exist in a space the world has yet to learn how to judge.

Bazaar

Naxal ka laal bikta hai, nashediyon ka maal bhi. Dihadi ka haq bikta hai, vidvaan ke vichaar bhi. Sansad ki seat bikti hai, gunegaar ka case bhi. Ye duniya nahi— bazaar hai. Yahan sab kuch bikta hai… Aur kabhi kabhi, insaan bhi.

Poetry

Magical Memoir

Enchanted by You You are the girl who can challenge a thousand butterflies. You are the light that heals the darkest sorrows. You are the spell to wake the magical forest. You are the only peace that echoes in eternity. You are the angel without wings, and I'm enchanted by you. ⸻ Spellbound Hand in hand, we slid across the rainbow, Time stood still as we danced in the moonlight. Challenged the wind, riding on a unicorn, Slept in a treehouse lit by countless fireflies. Loving her in this endless spell. ⸻ After the Magic The rainbow fades, and the winds turn cold. The moonlight no longer lights the path. The treehouse stands, but the fireflies are gone, And the unicorn, now a mere shadow in my mind. I still carry the echoes of her magic, but the spell is broken. Author's Note: An echo of love, told through the fading light of magic.
